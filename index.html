About Clau Cleaning Corp

Clean you can see. Safety you can trust.

Clau Cleaning Corp is a commercial cleaning company based in Dracut, Massachusetts. We specialize in doctor's offices, medical facilities and laboratories, and we also clean clinics, hospitals, offices, car dealerships, churches and schools, plus commercial carpet cleaning and floor care.

Every client gets a written work schedule with each area, task and frequency. Our crew works with color-coded microfiber, hospital-grade disinfectants, HEPA backpack vacuums and a color-coded flat mopping system, with a focus on high-touch surfaces to stop cross-contamination before it starts.

  • Trained, uniformed and background-checked staff
  • EPA-registered, hospital-grade disinfectants
  • Written work schedules and one-year service agreements
  • Quality inspections and a single point of contact

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you clean medical clinics and doctor's offices?

Yes. Doctor's offices, medical facilities and laboratories are our specialty. We clean and disinfect exam rooms, waiting areas, restrooms and offices for rehab hospitals, surgical and imaging centers and eye-care practices, using hospital-grade, EPA-registered disinfectants and protocols designed to prevent cross-contamination.

What other facilities do you clean?

Offices and professional buildings, car dealerships and showrooms, churches and parish halls, and public and private schools. We schedule the work after hours so your staff, customers and students are never interrupted.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Dracut, MA and serve Northern Massachusetts (Lowell, Lawrence, Methuen, Andover, North Andover, Haverhill, Newburyport, Billerica, Tewksbury, Chelmsford, Tyngsborough, Wilmington, Woburn and Burlington) and Southern New Hampshire (Nashua, Hudson, Salem, Derry, Londonderry, Manchester, Bedford, Merrimack, Pelham, Hollis and Amherst).

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the form below or call +1 978-328-2406. We schedule a free walkthrough of your facility and send a written quote, usually within 48 hours.

Do you offer commercial carpet cleaning and floor care?

Yes. We do hot water extraction and encapsulation for carpet, and stripping and waxing, scrub and recoat, machine scrubbing and burnishing for hard floors. Carpet and floor work is quoted per square foot.

Do you offer long-term maintenance contracts?

Yes. Most of our clients are on recurring daily, weekly or monthly plans with a dedicated crew and scheduled quality inspections.
